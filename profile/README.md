# Citco Construction, Inc.

Family-owned general contractor in Coral Gables, FL, specializing in residential additions, major renovations, kitchens, and bathrooms since 1982.

## What we build

- **Additions & Major Renovations** — room and second-story additions, whole-home renovations, structural and layout changes, permit coordination across Miami-Dade
- **Kitchen Remodels** — layout and cabinetry planning, countertops and appliances, plumbing and electrical rework
- **Bathroom Remodels** — primary suite remodels, guest and powder baths, tile, fixtures, and waterproofing
- **Owner's Rep / Pre-construction** — feasibility and estimating, permit expediting, vendor and trade management
